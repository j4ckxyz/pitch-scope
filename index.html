Reading the graph

In Pitch view each horizontal line is a semitone. A voice normally weaves around those lines, scooping into notes, drifting, adding vibrato. Pitch correction pulls the line flat onto the grid.

Cents off view subtracts the nearest note, so the whole performance collapses onto one scale. A corrected take hugs the zero line; an untreated one wanders across the full ±50 range.
